# Downloads & links

This page collects the most relevant software downloads for contributing music to *Night of the Meteor*.

Use it as a practical starting point, then align your exports with [File formats & specs](formats.md) and [Production pipeline](pipeline.md).

`*` = currently used by the music lead. The plugin list will grow during the sound-design stage.

## Start here (free-first baseline)

If you want a no-cost setup that still fits the workflow, start with:

1. **MuseScore** (writing and MIDI export)
2. **REAPER** (trial/evaluation) or **LMMS/Ardour** (free/open-source DAW options)
3. **Munt** (MT-32 emulation)
4. **Sforzando** plus a legally usable OPL-style soundfont

## Download safety and workflow fit

- Prefer **official vendor/project websites** over mirrors.
- Favor **stable releases** unless the team asks for beta builds.
- Keep installers/plugins documented in your cue notes for reproducibility.
- Before investing in a full toolchain, confirm deliverables still match [File formats & specs](formats.md).
